# Spec2Code — Design Risk Assessment Matrix (PLAN-084)

> Current phase **PLANNING / DESIGN**, not runtime risk remediation. This is a prioritization exercise based on known [risk register](.risk.md), source observations and PR #7 review. Scores are **provisional expert estimates**, not probabilities measured from incidents and not an approval to change code. A `P0` risk can remain BLOCKED after its mitigation is specified until verified tests and independent review occur.

## 1. Scoring model and classification

- **Likelihood L:** 1 Rare/limited exposure; 2 Unlikely; 3 Plausible; 4 Likely under adverse conditions; 5 Known reproducible defect or frequent exposure. State why the score applies.
- **Impact I:** 1 Cosmetic; 2 Minor localized inconvenience; 3 Material rework/functional regression; 4 Major workflow integrity, policy bypass or expensive recovery; 5 Unauthorized path/data loss, privacy/security breach or loss of critical audit integrity.
- **Exposure E = L × I**. P0 `16–25`, P1 `10–15`, P2 `5–9`, P3 `1–4`. Exact thresholds are **proposed** and require review. A statutory/mandatory security requirement remains blocking regardless score.
- **Status:** OPEN, ANALYZING, MITIGATION_DESIGNED, NEEDS_REVIEW, ACCEPTED_BY_OWNER, VERIFIED_MITIGATED or DEFERRED. **MITIGATION_DESIGNED ≠ VERIFIED_MITIGATED.**
- **Categories:** security/authorization, privacy, data integrity, state/workflow, policy/requirements, integration/compatibility, cross-platform/operational, observability/testing, migration, documentation/traceability and delivery.
- **Residual risk:** after design mitigation, record expected L/I **hypothetically**, not as an achieved reduction. Verification and acceptance evidence must come later from the authorized implementation/review phase.

## 4. Required per-run update procedure

After every **design run or review pass**, write one immutable dated row or handoff:

1. Re-read new source evidence and existing `RM-xxx` entries. Identify new, escalated, reduced or obsolete risks; preserve history.
2. Rescore `L` and `I` with a short rationale. Sort open items P0→P3, then exposure and irreversible impact; prevent cherry-picking low-risk tasks.
3. Choose the **highest unresolved relevant risk** and choose a response: `AVOID`, `MITIGATE`, `TRANSFER`, `ACCEPT` (independent owner only), `BLOCK` or `DEFER` with reason.
4. In the **current design phase**, correct the requirement, interface design, acceptance criteria, workflow model or test plan; update [RTM](.traceMatrix.md) and related design decision. A Python patch is an **implementation task to schedule**, not a design-pass action.
5. Record proposal evidence, owner, target review date if provided, affected requirement/AC IDs, and **expected residual risk** separately from verified residual status.
6. If new evidence worsens P0, stop downstream design signoff and return to P4/P5/P6 of the [planning workflow](.workflow/planningSpecificationWorkflow.md).
7. Handoff the exact outstanding risk and next smallest safe design action; close a risk only on actual observed verification and independent acceptance.
